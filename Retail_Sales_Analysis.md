# Business Question 1

## Calculate total revenue.

```sql
SELECT SUM(amount) AS total_revenue
FROM sales;
```

### Insight

Shows the overall revenue generated by the business.

---

# Business Question 2

## Find the average order value.

```sql
SELECT AVG(amount) AS average_order_value
FROM sales;
```

### Insight

Helps understand typical customer spending.

---

# Business Question 3

## Find total sales by product category.

```sql
SELECT p.category,
       SUM(s.amount) AS total_sales
FROM sales s
INNER JOIN products p
ON s.product_id = p.product_id
GROUP BY p.category
ORDER BY total_sales DESC;
```

### Insight

Identifies the best-performing product categories.

---

# Business Question 4

## Find the top 5 highest-selling products.

```sql
SELECT p.product_name,
       SUM(s.amount) AS total_sales
FROM sales s
INNER JOIN products p
ON s.product_id = p.product_id
GROUP BY p.product_name
ORDER BY total_sales DESC
LIMIT 5;
```

### Insight

Highlights products generating the most revenue.

---

# Business Question 5

## Find customers who spent more than £1,000.

```sql
SELECT c.customer_name,
       SUM(s.amount) AS total_spent
FROM customers c
INNER JOIN sales s
ON c.customer_id = s.customer_id
GROUP BY c.customer_name
HAVING SUM(s.amount) > 1000;
```

### Insight

Identifies high-value customers.

---

# Business Question 6

## Analyse monthly sales performance.

```sql
SELECT 
EXTRACT(MONTH FROM sale_date) AS sales_month,
SUM(amount) AS monthly_sales
FROM sales
GROUP BY sales_month
ORDER BY sales_month;
```

### Insight

Shows seasonal sales patterns.

---

# Business Question 7

## Rank customers by spending.

```sql
SELECT customer_name,
       total_spent,
       RANK() OVER
       (
       ORDER BY total_spent DESC
       ) AS customer_rank
FROM
(
SELECT c.customer_name,
       SUM(s.amount) AS total_spent
FROM customers c
JOIN sales s
ON c.customer_id = s.customer_id
GROUP BY c.customer_name
) customer_sales;
```

### Insight

Ranks customers based on their contribution to revenue.

---

# Business Question 8

## Create sales performance categories.

```sql
SELECT sale_id,
       amount,
       CASE
          WHEN amount >= 1000 THEN 'High Value'
          WHEN amount BETWEEN 500 AND 999 THEN 'Medium Value'
          ELSE 'Low Value'
       END AS sale_category
FROM sales;
```

### Insight

Classifies transactions for reporting purposes.
